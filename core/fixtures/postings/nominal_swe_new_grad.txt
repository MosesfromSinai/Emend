Software Engineer - New Grad (Summer 2026)
Nominal — New York, NY, USA (In-Office)
Compensation: $125K-$135K Annually

About Nominal
Nominal develops software to accelerate hardware development for spacecraft,
autonomous vehicles, and industrial machines. The platform simplifies data
analysis and integration for engineering teams. The company is backed by
Sequoia, General Catalyst, Founders Fund, Lux Capital, and Lightspeed
Ventures, and was featured in Forbes' next billion-dollar startups list.

The Role
The company seeks students graduating in Summer 2026 or sooner. New
graduates will work closely with world-class engineers solving high-impact
problems at an early-stage company with customers testing complex systems
like nuclear reactors and rockets.

Key Responsibilities
- Work with high-frequency, high-volume multi-modal hardware data infrastructure
- Design and build features collaborating with customers, sometimes in the field
- Own projects from start to finish with mentorship from experienced engineers
- Contribute to company growth and product expansion

Requirements/Qualifications
- Graduating Spring 2026 or sooner, seeking full-time position
- Personal passion for solving challenging problems
- Exceptional hands-on experience and strong core engineering skills through
  internships or projects
- Ambitious, resourceful, and perseverant traits

ITAR Requirements
Applicants must be U.S. citizens, nationals, permanent residents, refugees,
or asylees, or eligible for State Department authorization.
